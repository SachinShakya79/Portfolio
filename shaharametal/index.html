About the company

A practical craft studio for meaningful pieces.

Recognition should feel personal, considered and worthy of the occasion.

Shahara Metal Concern is a Kathmandu-based custom metal and presentation workshop creating pieces for schools, organizations, events, businesses and special occasions.

From a single name plate to a full set of awards, we can work from a reference photograph, sketch or simple idea and shape the final piece around your requirement.

Have a reference? Send a photo, sketch, quantity and approximate size when you enquire. It makes the conversation much easier.
